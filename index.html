📜 Ian's 27th Birthday Monopoly Rules

Welcome to Ian's 27th Birthday Monopoly! The ultimate goal is to become the wealthiest property tycoon and bankrupt your opponents while celebrating Ian's milestone!

1. Starting & Movement

  • Each player starts with $1,500 cash.
  • Roll two dice to move around the board. Passing or landing on GO collects $200.
  • Rolling Doubles grants you an extra turn! But roll 3 doubles in a row and you go straight to Jail!

2. Properties & Buildings

  • Land on unowned birthday properties (Music studios, U.P. Town Center hangouts, Manila dorm pads, Multimedia arts milestones) to buy them.
  • Collect full Color Sets (Monopolies) to double rent and unlock the ability to build Houses and Hotels for massive rent multipliers!

3. Special Spaces

  • Chance / Community Chest: Draw custom birthday cards for bonus cash, gifts, or penalties.
  • Go to Jail: Straight to birthday timeout! Pay $50 bail, roll doubles, or use a Get Out of Jail card to escape.
  • Tax Spaces: Pay birthday party taxes to the bank.

4. Winning the Game

  • Mortgage properties or sell houses if you run low on cash.
  • The last player standing with cash and assets wins Ian's 27th Birthday Championship! 🎂🎉
